---
title: Creating an Account
---

MapRoulette uses your OpenStreetMap account, so there is no separate account to create. If you don't have one yet, [sign up on OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/user/new).

To sign in, click **Sign in** on [MapRoulette](https://maproulette.org/). You'll be sent to OpenStreetMap to log in and allow MapRoulette to access your account, and then brought back to MapRoulette.

You can browse challenges without signing in, but you need to be signed in to work on tasks.
