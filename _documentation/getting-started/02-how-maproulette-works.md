---
title: How MapRoulette Works
---

MapRoulette breaks mapping work into small **Tasks**, each one a single thing to check or fix on the map, such as a missing road connection or a misspelled street name.

Tasks are grouped into **Challenges**. A challenge collects all the tasks of one kind, and has instructions explaining what to do. Challenges are grouped into **Projects**.

To get started:

1. [Find a challenge](/documentation/discovering-challenges/) you'd like to work on and click **Start Challenge**.
2. Read the instructions, look at the task on the map, and fix it in your [editor](/documentation/setting-your-default-osm-editor/) of choice.
3. Set the task's status: **Fixed**, **Not an Issue**, **Already Fixed** or **Can't Complete**. You can also skip a task and leave it for someone else.
4. MapRoulette gives you the next task, and you keep going.

You earn [points](/documentation/point-scoring/) for each task you complete.

Anyone can also [create their own challenge](/documentation/creating-a-challenge/).
