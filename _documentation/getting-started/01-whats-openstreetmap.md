---
title: What's OpenStreetMap?
---

[OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/) (OSM) is a free map of the world, built by volunteers and released under an open licence. Anyone can edit it, and anyone can use its data.

MapRoulette is a tool for fixing and improving OpenStreetMap. Every edit you make through MapRoulette is saved to OpenStreetMap, not to MapRoulette.

To learn more about OpenStreetMap and how to map, see:

- [About OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/about)
- [LearnOSM](https://learnosm.org/)
- [OpenStreetMap Wiki](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/)
- [OpenStreetMap US](https://openstreetmap.us/)
